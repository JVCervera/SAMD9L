# Sources and attribution

The authors are Alessandro Liquori and José Cervera. The supplementary workbook cites the reports and catalogues used to derive the study annotations.

AlphaFold Protein Structure Database (Google DeepMind and EMBL-EBI) supplies the SAMD9L reference model AF-Q8IVG5-F1 v6 and its predicted aligned error matrix. The distributed source files retain their recorded hashes. This work calculates inter-residue distances, confidence summaries and permutation contrasts from them. The database makes its data available under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). See the [AlphaFold DB FAQ](https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/faq).

The regulatory predictions and derived track summaries are from Google DeepMind AlphaGenome. The provider's research use conditions apply to these data. See the [AlphaGenome usage and citation guidance](https://google-deepmind-alphagenome.readthedocs-hosted.com/faqs.html#usage-licensing-and-support).
